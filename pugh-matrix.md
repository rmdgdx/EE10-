## Reading the result

The winning column is a starting point, not a verdict. Before deciding:

1. Can a losing concept's strongest `+` be grafted onto the winner?
2. Is any `−` fatal rather than merely unfavourable?
3. Re-run the matrix with the winner as the new datum. If the ranking flips,
   the criteria are not yet well defined.
