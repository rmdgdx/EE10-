## Worked examples of the required precision

| Not acceptable | Acceptable |
|---|---|
| "The device shall be accurate." | "≤ 5% MAPE against a calibrated reference over 0–200 A RMS." |
| "The device shall have long battery life." | "≥ 72 h on one charge at nominal duty cycle." |
| "The device shall be easy to use." | "Field setup by a non-specialist in ≤ 10 minutes using the supplied guide." |
| "The device shall be safe." | "Compliant creepage and clearance, fused input, insulated enclosure; verified by design review against PEC 2017 and a dielectric check." |
