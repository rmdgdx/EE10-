The Twelve Instructional Units

The twelve units map one-to-one onto the twelve teaching weeks. Weeks 5, 10, and 15 are examination and defence weeks and carry no new unit. Units 1–7 belong to Phase 1 — ideation, the research project proposal, and the scientometric or review paper submitted to an IEEE venue indexed in IEEE Xplore. Units 8–12 belong to Phase 2 — prototype execution, validation, and the full IEEE article.
