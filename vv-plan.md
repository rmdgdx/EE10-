**Team:** _______________  **Date:** _______________

*Verification* asks whether the device was built right — does it meet its
specifications? *Validation* asks whether the right device was built — does it
measure or do what it claims, judged against something trustworthy?

## 4. Analysis plan — declared before data collection

- [ ] Calibration curve and correction model; residuals reported
- [ ] MAE, RMSE, MAPE, R² against the reference
- [ ] Scatter plot against reference, plus residual plot
- [ ] Bland–Altman plot with bias and 95% limits of agreement
- [ ] Repeatability (%CV) from replicates; reproducibility across units or operators
- [ ] Warm-up and long-run drift
- [ ] Uncertainty budget with combined and expanded uncertainty
- [ ] Behaviour at temperature, humidity, and supply-disturbance extremes
